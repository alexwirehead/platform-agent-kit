## Helm charts

- Charts live in `deploy/helm/<chart>/` and follow `helm-conventions`.
- Check without a cluster: `dev/check-chart.sh` — `helm lint --strict` and `helm template` for every
  `ci/*-values.yaml`, then `kubeconform -strict`. Required values are checked with `expect_fail`
  cases in the same script: rendering without them must fail with a clear message.
- `README.md` and `values.schema.json` of a chart are generated by pre-commit hooks
  (`.pre-commit-config.yaml`), never edited by hand.
- Helm template syntax and functions come from the official Helm docs, not from memory.
